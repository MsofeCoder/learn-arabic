# LisanFlow MVP Seed Content

## Editorial note
This seed is based on the project's Dawrah preparation plan and the type of formal Islamic lecture Arabic represented by the supplied reference materials. It is **not** a transcript of the supplied videos and must not be presented as one.

## Active program
**Slug:** `dawrah-arabic-prep`

**Title:** Dawrah Arabic Preparation

**Audience:** beginner/basic Arabic learner

**Target:** improve recognition of formal Islamic lecture phrases, core vocabulary, and listening structure.

## Day 1 — Lecture foundation
Objective: recognize common lecture transitions and evidence markers.

Tasks:
1. Phrase recognition — 5 min
2. Core Islamic vocabulary — 7 min
3. Focused listening — 8 min
4. Recall review — 5 min

## Day 2 — Listening structure
Objective: identify topic, evidence, explanation, and conclusion.

Tasks:
1. Listening pass — 10 min
2. Phrase spotting — 5 min
3. Vocabulary review — 7 min

## Day 3 — Grammar unlockers
Objective: recognize negation, connectors, and relative structures.

Tasks:
1. Connector drill — 7 min
2. Listening with structure notes — 10 min
3. Flashcard review — 8 min

## Day 4 — Islamic Arabic specialization
Objective: recognize creed/hadith vocabulary in continuous speech.

Tasks:
1. Aqeedah vocabulary — 10 min
2. Listening — 10 min
3. Retrieval review — 7 min

## Day 5 — Mock Dawrah lecture
Objective: practice capturing meaning without translating every word.

Tasks:
1. 20–30 minute lecture simulation — 20 min
2. Recall + review — 8 min
